Questions

Good to know.

Quick answers before you sign in.

Who is Agile Hub for?

Organizations that coordinate hubs, products, sales, staff and beneficiary businesses and want those records together in one role-aware system.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your Agile Hub administrator. Reach out to them, or use the contact details below, for access or onboarding.

Can one hub see another hub's records?

Records are hub-aware. What each person can see depends on their role and the hubs they belong to.

Is there a history of changes?

Yes. Important changes and financial actions are kept in an audit trail so you can see what happened.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Agile Hub runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.
