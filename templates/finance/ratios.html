Ratio Analysis Guide

Debt to Assets Measures the proportion of total assets financed by creditors. Ratios below 0.40 generally indicate strong solvency.
Liabilities to Equity Evaluates capital structure and how much debt is used to finance assets relative to shareholder value.
ROA & ROE Profitability indicators demonstrating how efficiently management generates earnings from assets and equity investments.

Ratios use recorded asset, liability, equity, and operational transaction values. Missing or zero denominators are shown as unavailable.
